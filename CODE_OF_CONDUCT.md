# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

We are committed to a respectful, inclusive, technically rigorous community. Constructive disagreement is welcome; harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, doxxing, and deliberately disruptive behavior are not.

Contributors should:

- discuss ideas and evidence rather than people;
- give actionable, proportionate review feedback;
- acknowledge uncertainty and correct mistakes openly;
- protect private source code, credentials, and personal information;
- respect maintainers' scope, security, and release decisions.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainers through GitHub. Maintainers may edit or remove content, close discussions, reject contributions, or temporarily or permanently restrict participation when necessary to protect the community.

Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical. Retaliation against someone who reports a concern in good faith is prohibited.
